❓ QnA

What's the difference from Signal or Telegram?
Signal and Telegram are "apps" providing communication services. Numotirus is a "protocol" and "library". You compile it yourself. You use it yourself.
How to add friends?
Exchange keys. You give me your key, I give you mine. No central server to search for anyone.
How do messages work without a server?
P2P direct connection. Your device talks directly to the other device. No server in between.
Can non-programmers use it?
Currently requires compilation. In the future, community member might provide a GUI installer.
How do you comply with social media age restrictions?
We do not operate a service. We only publish code. The law targets "platforms", not "programming tools".
Can I use it right now?
No, you can't. But you can try to use best practices, such as Signal. We don't do better than them, at least now we don't.
